Title: The Shadow Protocol (ពិភពស្រមោលសម្ងាត់)
Genre: Sci-Fi Action Thriller

Plot Synopsis:
A disgraced former detective named Alex discovers an underground cryogenic laboratory hidden beneath the subway system. While investigating a missing cyber-scientist, he uncovers that a powerful megacorporation is erasing memories of citizens and replacing them with obedient synthetic clones.

When Alex is framed for the murder of the city governor, he has only 24 hours to team up with a rogue AI hacker named Maya to expose the conspiracy before the entire city is put into a permanent digital lockdown.
